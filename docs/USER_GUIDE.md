# Phoenix Inventory: a one-page guide for the boss and HR

You can **look at everything and print reports**. You cannot change anything: only Sahan (IT) does that, so there is nothing here you can break.

## Logging in
1. Open the address Sahan gave you (it works on a laptop or a phone; on a laptop you can also "Install" it from the browser's address bar so it opens like a program).
2. Sign in with the email and password Sahan made for you. There is no "forgot password" email: if you forget it, ask Sahan to reset it.
3. If you see **"Ask IT for access"**, your account has no permission yet. Tell Sahan.

## Home (the first page)
- **Needs attention** at the top: one short list of what is overdue, running low or waiting on IT. Red lines are overdue or empty; amber ones are coming up. Alike items are grouped ("12 Laptop warranties end in 18 days"). Click a line to see the details.
- **In Use / Spare / Faulty / Lost / Active**: counts of everything the company owns. "Active" means not retired or thrown away.
- **Spare pool by category**: a red row means there are fewer spare ones than IT wants to keep (for example no spare laptop to give a new joiner).
- **Assets by department**: what each department has. Click a department for its list.
- **All reminders** at the bottom (click to open): every warranty ending and service due in the next 30 days. A red row is overdue.

## The menu
The left menu (on a phone: the bar at the bottom, and **More** for the rest) is in groups: **Daily work** (Faults, Service jobs), **Records** (Assets, People, Stock) and **Office** (Reports, Export to Excel, Settings). The red number next to Faults is how many are open.

## Finding things
- **Assets**: every item, with who has it and which department it belongs to. Type in the search box (tag like `PBS-LAP-001`, make, model, name). The filters narrow it down. **Export to Excel** gives the same list as a spreadsheet.
- **Group by** on the Assets page shows the list in groups: by **Department** (what Sales has), by **Person** (what one person holds, with their accessories), by Category or by Status. Click a group to open it.
- **People**: every staff member, with the items they hold. You can see their National ID (NID) numbers, so please treat screens and printouts as confidential.
- Click any item or person for the full history of what happened to it. An item's page says first who has it and its department. Everything someone holds belongs to their department.
- **Reports**: service history by vendor (every service job, grouped by who did the work), with **Export to Excel**.

## Printing a leaver clearance sheet (HR)
1. Open **People**, click the person who is leaving.
2. Click **Leaver clearance sheet**. A new tab opens with a one-page form listing everything they still hold, with a box to tick for each item and its accessories as they come back, and signature lines for the employee, IT and HR.
3. Click **Print or save as PDF** and print on A4.
4. IT ticks the items off as they come in, and the person signs. The original goes to IT, the copy to HR. Sahan then marks the person as Left in the system.

## What you will not see
- Buttons like Issue, Return, Add, Edit, Log a fault or More actions. They are for IT only.
- Importing from Excel, which is IT-only.

## Something looks wrong?
Tell Sahan. If a page will not load, check the internet connection first: the page says "Could not reach the server" when that is the problem.
